WSDC Points Summary

Registry points awarded by event, division, and placement — not full competition results.

How to read this page
  • This shows points awarded to dancers, not full event results.
  • Events are grouped by the date results were posted.
  • Click an event to expand and see divisions (Champions, All-Star, Advanced).
  • Placements show Leaders & Followers; medals (🥇🥈🥉) mark 1st, 2nd, 3rd.
  • Finalists — couples in the final without a specific placement.
  • (+X) — Registry points awarded for that placement.
  • [N] — total points in that division after this event.
  • 🟡 — reached ALLOWED threshold (dancer can now compete in the next division).
  • 🟢 — reached REQUIRED threshold (dancer must now compete in the next division) or earned first points in Intermediate/Advanced/All-Stars.
