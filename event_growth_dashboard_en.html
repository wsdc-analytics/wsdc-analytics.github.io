Methodology

  • Metric. Competitors are Leader + Follower headcounts summed across Newcomer, Novice, Intermediate, Advanced, All-Star, and Champion. We use the competitions dump when enough skill divisions have complete counts. If not, we use a Chart-5 tier midpoint (~). A partial dump is never shown as exact.
  • Comparison. Each edition in the selected year is compared with that event's previous scored edition. If exactly one calendar year was skipped, we still compare and mark "after 1-year break". If two or more years were skipped, the status is Restarted (no %). No previous edition means New.
  • Status. Growing or Declining only when |%| is at least 10% and |Δ| is at least 10. Smaller moves stay Stable.
  • Regions. Comparable competitors are summed (previous to current). Region Growing/Declining uses the same thresholds as events. If a region is not Declining overall but still has declining events inside, it shows a "declining inside" flag so redistribution does not hide them.
