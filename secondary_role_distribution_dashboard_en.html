Methodology

Definitions

Primary role is the role in which a dancer holds more points in a higher Skill Level division. Secondary role is the other role.

Secondary-role points are points earned in the selected role by dancers whose primary role is different. Secondary share is those points divided by all points in the same year / role / division slice.

How to read the chart

Each bubble is a country. The horizontal axis is secondary-role share (%). The vertical axis is secondary-role points. Bubble size is the number of dancers who scored in a secondary role in that slice.

Dashed lines mark the average share and average points across the countries currently plotted. Click a bubble, then View dancers, to open the list behind it. Hide USA removes the United States so other countries are easier to compare.

Data coverage

Snapshots cover 2023–2026 Skill Level points from the WSDC Points Registry. The Updated date next to the title is the data cut-off for the latest refresh (including a partial current year when that year is selected).
