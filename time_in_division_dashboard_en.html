Methodology

  • Spell: one dancer × division × role (Leader/Follower separately).
  • Start: first event-month with a point in that spell.
  • Done (May / Must): first month the rules threshold is reached for that era (allowed / required). All-Stars: before formal Champions rules (pre-2021) — Champions points only (1 may / 10 must) on real events; from 2021 — OR of Champions pts or All-Stars pts.
  • Months: inclusive calendar months from first through done (same month = 1; Nov→Mar = 5). Day-of-month is unknown, so we count months touched, not a zero-based gap.
  • Events: unique event editions (name + year-month) in that division×role up to and including the crossing for the selected threshold. Points and months always start from the first point in the spell — including years before the dashboard year floor.
  • Window (Scatter / Density / Table): display filter only — done year in From–To, within the allowed/required era (Novice / Intermediate / Advanced from 2018; All-Stars from 2021). The floor does not restart the clock or drop earlier events from the count. Earlier single-threshold done years are hidden so the chart focuses on the may/must corridor. Data cut-off: —.
  • Scatter: one point per spell (rings when many share a cell). Hover shows one dancer. Dashed lines = median months / events. KPI “≤ both medians” = share in the lower-left of the median cross.
  • Density: darker / larger = more dancers in a bin (default 6 months × 2 events). Dashed lines = medians.
  • Dynamics: line of one KPI by calendar year of done in that same era (full era span; From/To hidden). KPI cards choose the metric. The Role control becomes a view mode: Overall = one combined line; Leaders & Followers = two lines to compare roles.
  • Dynamics gaps: a missing point is not “no median in the data” — that year’s sample was too small to plot a stable summary. Dancers plots every year with at least one spell. Median months, Median events, and ≤ both plot only when that year has at least 10 spells (Overall: combined count; Leaders & Followers: each line uses its own role count). Years below that threshold are left blank and the line breaks. Sparse divisions (e.g. early All-Stars years) often show a few isolated points rather than a continuous line — switch to Dancers to see every year with activity.
  • KPIs: Dancers = spell count in the current filter; Median months / Median events = medians; ≤ both = share at or below both medians (lower-left of the median cross). On Dynamics, click a card to choose the plotted metric (KPIs always summarize both roles).
  • Table: same filtered spells, sorted months ↑ then events ↑ then name; light role tint; CSV exports the full filtered set (includes threshold).
  • Median guides (Scatter/Density): dashed lines at median months / events; axis limits include medians and full spell range with headroom so edge points stay visible.
  • May→MUST corridor (JT-3): among spells that reached May, buffer steps p25/p50/p75 = first month at allowed + fraction×(required−allowed) points; Must = required crossing when present. Dynamics: among each May done year cohort, share who later reached each buffer step (n≥10 to plot).
  • Division pause (JT-2): inclusive months from last point in division D to first in D+1 (same role). Division filter = from division (All-Stars → Champions entry). Overlap spells flagged; median pause excludes overlaps. Year window uses entry (first_ym) year — no May/Must floor.
  • All-Stars qualify (JN-1b): yearly counts and cumulative totals — Advanced allowed (eligible) vs first All-Stars point (entered). Not split by role.
