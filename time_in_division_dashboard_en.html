Methodology

  • Spell: one dancer × division × role (Leader/Follower separately).
  • Start: first event-month with a point in that spell.
  • Done (May / Must): first month the rules threshold is reached for that era (allowed / required). All-Stars: before formal Champions rules (pre-2021) — Champions points only (1 may / 10 must) on real events; from 2021 — OR of Champions pts or All-Stars pts. No synthetic date at rule introduction.
  • Months: calendar months between year-months (same formula as division-transition metrics).
  • Events: unique events with a point in that division×role over the whole career.
  • Window (Scatter / Density / Table): done year in From–To, only within the allowed/required era for that division (Novice / Intermediate / Advanced from 2018; All-Stars from 2021). Earlier single-threshold years are excluded — this dashboard focuses on the may/must buffer corridor. Data cut-off: —.
  • Scatter: one point per spell (rings when many share a cell). Hover shows one dancer. Dashed lines = median months / events. KPI “≤ both medians” = share in the lower-left of the median cross.
  • Density: darker / larger = more dancers in a bin (default 6 months × 2 events). Dashed lines = medians.
  • Dynamics: line of one KPI by calendar year of done in that same era (full era span; From/To hidden). Click a KPI card to choose the series (resets to Dancers when entering Dynamics). Years with 0 dancers create a gap. Months / Events / ≤ both require at least 10 dancers in that year.
  • KPIs: Dancers = spell count in the current filter; Months / Events = medians; ≤ both = share at or below both medians (lower-left of the median cross).
  • Table: same filtered spells, sorted months ↑ then events ↑ then name; light role tint; typeahead jumps to a row without filtering; CSV exports the full filtered set (includes threshold).
  • Median guides (Scatter/Density): dashed lines at median months / events; axis limits include medians and full spell range with headroom so edge points stay visible.
