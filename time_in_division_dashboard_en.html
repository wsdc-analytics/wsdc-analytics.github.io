Methodology
  • Spell: one dancer × division × role (Leader/Follower separately).
  • Start: first event-month with a point in that spell.
  • Done (May / Must): first month the rules threshold is reached for that era (allowed / required). All-Stars uses champions_allowed / champions_required (OR of Champions pts or All-Stars pts).
  • Months: calendar months between year-months (same formula as division-transition metrics). Day-level start dates are not required.
  • Events (Y): unique events with a point in that division×role over the whole career.
  • Window: keep spells whose done year falls in the selected From–To year range (inclusive). Same year in both = that calendar year only. Data cut-off: —.
  • Density bins: fixed 6 months × 2 events.
  • Only completed spells (threshold already reached) are plotted. Overlapping values are fanned on a small ring around the true point.
