About this page

How to read
  • This page covers New Champions crossings from 2025 onward (earlier years are not included).
  • One card = one threshold crossing for one dancer in one role (leader or follower).
  • Allowed — cumulative All-Stars points reach 150+.
  • Required — All-Stars reach 225+, or Champions reach 10+.
  • Click a card to expand totals and path details (first points, counts, top events/cities, continents).
  • Each year row shows quiet role×status totals (L/F + A/R) for that calendar year.
