Which divisions do WSDC events run each year — and how big are those fields? This tool counts, year by year, how many events held Newcomer through Champions. Open any division to see how often its Leader and Follower fields reached each tier, from Tier 1 (5–10 competitors) to Tier 6 (130+). Narrow it down by years and location, switch between Count and Share, and click any cell to see the events behind the number. The ⓘ buttons and Methodology explain each number.

Methodology

What this shows

Every row is one year, and every event is counted once for each year it took place — a series that runs every year appears in every year. For each event we note which divisions it held and how big each field was. Leaders and Followers are counted separately, because the same Novice can draw very different numbers of Leaders and Followers.

The tool opens on Divisions: how many events held Newcomer, Novice, Intermediate, Advanced, All-Star and Champions each year. Click a division name to open its tiers — one column per tier for Leaders and one for Followers. ← All divisions takes you back; the drop-down next to it switches straight to another division.

Example. Austin Swing Dance Championships 2018 held Novice with 47 Leaders and 87 Followers. In the Divisions view it adds one to Novice · 2018. In the Novice tiers view it adds one to Leader Tier 4 and one to Follower Tier 5.

The data starts in 2018. Years with no events in the current filter are left out.

Tiers

The WSDC gives every competition a tier based on how many dancers competed in one role. The bigger the field, the higher the tier — and the more points the finalists earn.

  • Tier 1 — 5–10 competitors
  • Tier 2 — 11–19
  • Tier 3 — 20–39
  • Tier 4 — 40–79
  • Tier 5 — 80–129
  • Tier 6 — 130 or more

Leaders and Followers get their own tier, so one Novice can be Tier 4 for Leaders and Tier 5 for Followers at the same event.

Count & Share

Count is the number of events. Share turns it into a percentage, and what it is a percentage of depends on the view:

  • In Divisions — of all events that year in the current filter.
  • In tiers — of the events that year that held this division for this role.

Example. In 2024 there were 139 events, and 113 of them held All-Star: Count 113 · Share 81.3%. Open Advanced: 135 events held Advanced for Leaders, and 54 of those were Tier 3 — Count 54 · Share 40%.

Colors

Each year is shaded on its own: the darkest cell in a row is the division or tier that came up most often that year. This makes it easy to see what was typical in a given year, but it is not meant for comparing years with each other — for that, read the numbers.

In the tiers view, Leaders are blue and Followers are pink.

Location filters

Pick a Continent, then a Country; for the United States a State filter appears as well. Each list starts with the places that hosted the most events in the selected years. Location is where the event took place that year, so a series that has since stopped still keeps its country.

Event lists

Hover a cell to see its count and share. Click a cell to open the list of events behind it, with city, country and dates. The small tags in the corner of each card show how many dancers competed: 42L means 42 Leaders, 67F means 67 Followers. For a few events the exact numbers are not available, and the tags are left out.

An empty cell means there were no such events that year. A dash in Share means there was nothing to compare with: no event that year held this division for this role.
